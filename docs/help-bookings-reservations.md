# Bookings & reservations

Use Jollof Living to find a residence, check availability, reserve your dates, and manage the stay from one place.

## Make a reservation

1. Open **Stays** and select a residence. Review the location, capacity, amenities, house rules, cancellation policy, and whether it is Instant Book.
2. Select check-in and check-out dates and the number of guests. Check-out must be after check-in, and the residence must have capacity for your party.
3. Choose any available add-ons and apply a promotion or gift card if you have one. The checkout total is shown in NGN before you submit.
4. Enter your name and valid email address, then select **Complete reservation**. Jollof Living uses Paystack for the booking payment.
5. Instant Book reservations are confirmed when payment is completed. For a residence that requires approval, the booking is sent as a request and remains pending until the host responds.

## After you book

Your confirmation page and email contain the reservation reference, dates, residence, amount, and next steps. Sign in and open **Trips** to see upcoming, active, past, or cancelled reservations. Keep the reference available when contacting support.

Payments are held in escrow. The reservation page shows the payment state; do not send payment directly to a host. On an eligible stay, check-in becomes available on the arrival day and check-out completes the stay and releases escrow according to the platform workflow.

## Changes and cancellations

Open the reservation in **Trips** and use the available action to request a date or guest change, or to cancel. The cancellation policy belongs to the residence and is shown before booking. Flexible, moderate, and strict policies can return different amounts depending on how close the cancellation is to check-in. The reservation page is the source of truth for the policy and refund estimate.

For stays of 30 nights or more, split payment may be available: 50% at booking and 50% at check-in. The option appears only when the stay qualifies.

## If something goes wrong

Use Help centre live chat for a quick answer. For a stay issue, open the dispute centre and include your reservation reference, a clear description, and relevant evidence. In an emergency, use local emergency services first.

## Important notes

- Never share your password, payment details, or one-time codes.
- A booking request is not a confirmed reservation until the host approves it.
- Availability can change while you are checking out; if dates are taken, choose another window.
- Jollof Living records reservation amounts in NGN, even when display preferences show another currency.
